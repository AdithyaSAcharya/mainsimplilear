
Students can explore courses, learn through videos and quizzes, track progress, and earn certificates.
Instructors can create, manage, and publish engaging courses while interacting directly with their learners.
Super Admins oversee the entire platform with full control over users, courses, and analytics.


# LearnStack Role & Permissions Matrix

This document outlines the three primary roles within the LearnStack platform and the specific actions they are authorized to perform.

## 1. STUDENT
*The default role assigned when a user signs up on the platform.*

**Capabilities:**
* **Course Discovery:** Can view the public course catalog, read short descriptions, and see course metadata (like the author's name).
* **Enrollment:** Can enroll in any published course.
* **Course Consumption:** 
  * Can access lessons, video content, and reading materials for courses they are enrolled in.
  * Can take quizzes associated with their enrolled courses.
  * Can track their own progress (completed vs. pending lessons).
* **Certification:** Automatically receives a completion certificate once 100% of the lessons and quizzes in an enrolled course are finished.
* **Communication:** Can initiate real-time chat messages with the instructor of any enrolled course.

**Restrictions:**
* Cannot access the course creation or editing pages.
* Cannot view the admin dashboard or provision other users.
* Cannot view content of courses they are not enrolled in.

---

## 2. INSTRUCTOR
*A specialized role manually provisioned by a Super Admin.*

**Capabilities:**
* **All Student Privileges:** Instructors can act as students (enroll in and consume other courses).
* **Course Authoring:**
  * Can create new courses, set thumbnails, and write descriptions.
  * Can add, edit, or delete video lessons and text content for **their own** courses.
  * Can build interactive quizzes and attach them to specific lessons.
* **Course Publishing:** Can toggle their authored courses from "Draft" to "Published" so students can enroll.
* **Analytics & Management:** 
  * Has access to an "Instructor Panel" to track all of their authored courses.
  * Can view the list of students actively enrolled in their courses.
* **Communication:** Can read and reply to direct chat messages from students enrolled in their courses.

**Restrictions:**
* Cannot edit, manage, or delete courses authored by *other* instructors.
* Cannot access the Super Admin control panel.
* Cannot provision other instructors or view global platform metrics.

---

## 3. SUPER_ADMIN
*The highest-level platform administrator. This role is typically hardcoded or assigned directly in the database.*

**Capabilities:**
* **Unrestricted Platform Access:** Can bypass enrollment locks and view the internal content (videos/lessons) of *any* course on the platform, published or draft.
* **Global Course Management:** Can inspect, edit, or delete *any* course created by any instructor.
* **User Provisioning:** Has exclusive rights to create/provision new Instructor accounts via the Admin Dashboard.
* **Platform Analytics (Admin Dashboard):**
  * Can view global platform metrics (Total Students, Total Instructors, Total Courses).
  * Has access to the Platform Courses Registry (monitoring published vs. draft ratios).
  * Can access the "Student Directory" to inspect individual student profiles and their enrollment status.
  * Can access the "Instructor Directory" to see instructor profiles and their authored courses.

**Restrictions:**
* *None.* The Super Admin has full overarching control of both content moderation and user management.
